## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Personal Data (Provided by You)

When you register and use InVoice, we collect the following personal information, primarily through your Google Sign-In authentication:

- **Full Name** — used to personalise your account and populate invoice sender details.
- **Email Address** — used for account identification, communication, and support.
- **Profile Picture** — retrieved from your Google account and displayed within the App.

### 2.2 Business Data (Provided by You)

To facilitate invoice generation, you may voluntarily provide:

- **Business Name** — used as the sender identity on invoices.
- **Business Logo** — image files uploaded by you for inclusion on invoices (InVoice Pro feature).
- **Payment Terms** — such as due dates, late fees, and payment instructions you configure.
- **Invoice Data** — including client names, line-item descriptions, amounts, tax details, and invoice numbers generated through the App.
- **Custom Invoice Prefixes and Numbering** — configured by InVoice Pro subscribers.

### 2.3 Device and Usage Data (Collected Automatically)

When you use the App, we automatically collect certain technical information, including:

- **Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) Tokens** — device-specific tokens used to deliver push notifications and automated invoice reminders.
- **Device Type and Model** — used for technical support and App optimisation.
- **Operating System and Version** — used for compatibility and debugging purposes.
- **App Usage Analytics** — aggregated, non-identifiable data about feature usage to improve the App (e.g., frequency of invoice creation, feature adoption).

### 2.4 Payment Data

We do **not** directly collect or store your payment card details, bank account numbers, or mobile money credentials. Payment transactions are processed exclusively by our third-party payment processors, **Paystack** and **Google Pay**. Please refer to their respective privacy policies for details on how they handle your financial data.

## 4. AI Data Processing

### 4.1 Google Gemini Integration

InVoice uses **Google Gemini**, an artificial intelligence service provided by Google LLC, to parse and extract structured invoice information from text you provide within the App.

When you use the AI invoice creation feature:

- The text content you submit is transmitted to Google Gemini's API for processing.
- Google Gemini processes this input to identify and extract invoice fields such as client names, services, amounts, and due dates.
- The extracted data is returned to the App and used solely to populate your invoice.

### 4.2 Data Security in AI Processing

All data transmitted to Google Gemini is sent over encrypted connections (TLS/HTTPS). We rely on Google's enterprise-grade security infrastructure for the processing of this data, as governed by Google's API Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

### 4.3 Important Limitations and User Responsibility

- **Do not submit highly sensitive, classified, or confidential financial information** through the AI parsing feature. This includes trade secrets, privileged financial strategies, or any information that, if disclosed to a third party, could cause harm.
- The AI parsing feature is designed for routine commercial invoice generation. It is **not** designed to handle classified governmental, legal, or sensitive corporate financial information.
- We make no warranties regarding the accuracy or completeness of AI-parsed data. You are solely responsible for reviewing and verifying all AI-generated invoice content before sending it to clients.
- Data submitted for AI processing may be subject to Google's own data usage policies. We encourage you to review the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [Google Gemini API Terms of Service](https://ai.google.dev/terms).

## 6. Data Retention

We retain your personal data and business data for as long as your account is active or as necessary to provide you with the Services.

Specifically:
- **Account Data** (name, email, profile picture) is retained for the lifetime of your account.
- **Invoice and Business Data** is retained until you delete it within the App or request account deletion.

## 7. Account and Data Deletion

You have the right to request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time.

**To request deletion:**

1. Email us at **[hello.invoiceapp@gmail.com](mailto:hello.invoiceapp@gmail.com)** with the subject line: **"Data Deletion Request"**.
2. Include the email address associated with your InVoice account.
3. We will process your request within **30 days** and confirm deletion via email.

Upon deletion:
- Your account profile, business data, and invoice history will be permanently deleted from our Firebase Firestore database.
- FCM tokens associated with your account will be invalidated.
- Anonymised, aggregated analytics data that cannot identify you may be retained.
- Financial transaction records required by law may be retained for the legally required period.
